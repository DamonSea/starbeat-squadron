Pick a track, then shoot on the beat

A Galaga-style shooter where the soundtrack is the trigger. Your cannon only fires when you hit the beat. The swarm sways, dives and shoots in time with the music, and every wave you clear pushes the tempo up 8 BPM. Faster tracks mean faster dives. Your uploaded tracks are real recordings, so they keep their tempo (on Moon Colony, Primate Pulse and Final Transmission, half-beats count as shots too, and Red Sky is charted note by note to the song's actual hits, like a Guitar Hero chart), waves get more aggressive instead, and surviving to the end of the song wins.

Every 8 on-beat shots raises your multiplier, up to ×8. Divers are worth double, and the bosses at the top take two hits. Every third stage is a boss fight against one of four skulls (the Skull Ship, Skull King, Demon Skull and Ghost Skull), each firing on the beat. When a red outline flashes under it, get out of the way: if the tractor beam from its jaw catches you, land 4 Perfect shots in a row to break free and stun it, or lose a ship. Fire when the shrinking ring closes on your ship and the markers meet in the bottom gauge.
