STARBEATSQUADRON

Shoot on the beat, or don't shoot at all

A Galaga-style shooter where the soundtrack is the trigger. Your cannon only fires when you hit the beat. The swarm sways, dives and shoots in time with the music, and every wave you clear pushes the tempo up 8 BPM.

PERFECTWithin 55 ms: twin piercing shots, plus a spread at ×4

MISSOff the beat: the cannon jams and your combo resets

Every 8 on-beat shots raises your multiplier, up to ×8. Divers are worth double, and the bosses at the top take two hits. Fire when the shrinking ring closes on your ship and the markers meet in the bottom gauge.

If your shots keep grading late (common with Bluetooth headphones), slide the offset right.
